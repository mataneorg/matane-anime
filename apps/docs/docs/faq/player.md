# Player

## Which formats play?

HLS and MP4. Other containers and codecs depend on what your system's Chromium build supports. A stream that cannot be played gives a clear error with actions instead of a blank screen.

## How does the player choose a stream?

The best candidate first: your last manual choice for that anime, then your preferred quality, then the server that worked last. If it fails, the app moves to the next at the same position, with a short notice, up to three times. See [Watching and progress](/docs/guides/watching).

## How do I change the quality or server?

Use the server and quality menu in the player. A manual choice is remembered for that anime.

## When is an episode marked as watched?

At 85% by default; change it in **Settings, Player** (50% to 100%). Going back does not undo it. See [Progress](/docs/guides/watching#progress).

## Why does an episode start a few seconds before where I stopped?

If you stopped more than 10 seconds in and before the watched threshold, it resumes 3 seconds before. Episodes already watched, or barely started, begin at the start.

## Can I change the keyboard shortcuts?

Yes: **Settings, Player, Keyboard shortcuts**. See [Player settings](/docs/guides/player-settings#changing-a-shortcut).

## Can I turn off autoplay?

Yes, in **Settings, Player**. You can also cancel the countdown when it appears.

## Does the screen stay on?

Yes, while the player is playing. Media keys work.

## Can I play at another speed?

Yes, from 0.5x to 2x; the choice is remembered. Use <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd>.

## Why is nothing recorded in my history?

[Incognito](/docs/guides/incognito) may be on. It shows in the title bar and in the player.
