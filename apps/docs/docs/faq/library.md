# Library

## How do I add an anime to my library?

Open it from **Browse** and choose **Add to library**. See [Getting started](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-anime-to-your-library).

## What happens to my progress if I remove an anime?

Its progress and history stay; you can add it again later. Only the library entry is removed.

## How do I sort or filter the library?

Use the controls above the library: filter by unwatched, started, downloaded, status or source, and sort by title, last watched, latest episode, date added or number of unwatched episodes. See [Library](/docs/guides/library#organising).

## Can an anime be in more than one category?

Yes, or in none. See [Categories](/docs/guides/categories).

## Why is an episode I watched shown as "Removed by source"?

The source stopped listing it. The app keeps any episode you watched, started or downloaded, or have in history, and an empty list from a source never deletes anything.

## What does the number on a cover mean?

How many episodes you have not watched.

## How do I move an anime to another source?

See [Source migration](/docs/guides/source-migration).

## Is my library saved if I uninstall the app?

Yes: uninstalling does not delete your data. Make a [backup](/docs/guides/backups) before moving to another computer.

## Why are the display, filters and sort remembered, but not the search or the category tab?

The display, cover size, sort and filters are how you like your library to look, so they are kept. The category tab and the search text are temporary and are not.
