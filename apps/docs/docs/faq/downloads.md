# Downloads

## What can be downloaded?

HLS (video on demand, including AES-128 encrypted segments and separate audio) and MP4. A live stream is refused with a clear message. See [Downloads and offline](/docs/guides/downloads).

## Where are my downloads?

By default in `Documents/Matane Anime`, as `<folder>/<Source (LANG)>/<Anime>/<Episode>/`. Change it in **Settings, Downloads**; the app offers to move what you already downloaded. **Settings, Data and storage, Open folder** opens it.

## Why did a download fail?

A failed download shows an error with a retry button, and what is already downloaded is kept, so a retry fetches only what is missing. Failed segments are retried three times. The queue waits while you are offline. Common reasons it is refused: it would pass your size limit, there is not enough free space, the source's extension is not installed, it is a live stream, or the source offers no stream.

## Can I download more than one episode at a time?

Yes, 1 to 3 (**Settings, Downloads, Episodes at once**). Each source's rate limit still applies.

## Does the queue survive closing the app?

Yes, it is persistent and continues after you reopen the app. You can pause or resume everything or one item, reorder, cancel or delete.

## What is the size limit?

20 GB by default. When downloads pass it, automatic downloads stop and manual ones ask first.

## Can episodes delete themselves after I watch them?

Yes: **Settings, Downloads, Delete after watching**, with categories you can keep. Off by default.

## What if I delete a file by hand?

The download is marked **broken** and the app streams the episode instead.

## Can I watch downloaded episodes elsewhere?

Streams that arrive as segments are saved as a folder with a playlist; single files are saved as one MP4. They are meant to be played by Matane Anime.
