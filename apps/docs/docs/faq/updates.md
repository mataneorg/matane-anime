# Updates

This page is about **new episodes**. For updates of the app itself see [Updating](/docs/guides/install#updating); for extensions see [Extensions and repositories](/docs/guides/extensions#installing-updating-and-removing).

## How often does the app check for new episodes?

Every 12 hours by default. Change it in **Settings, Library, New episode checks**: off, every 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours, or weekly. It also checks when the app opens if the interval has passed. See [New episodes](/docs/guides/updates).

## Why did a new episode not show up?

- It only counts as new if the app found it **after** you added the anime to your library.
- Library and category checks skip completed anime, anime you never started and anime with many unwatched episodes (all configurable). Checking one anime ignores these rules.
- The app has to be running. Turn on **Start with your computer** and **Close to the system tray** for background checks.
- The source may be down or the extension out of date: look for an error on the Updates page.

## Can I check by hand?

Yes: **Check now** on the Updates page, **Check for updates** in the [command palette](/docs/guides/command-palette), or from a category or one anime.

## What is the difference between "Mark as seen" and "Mark as watched"?

**Mark as seen** only takes the episode off the Updates list and the badge; it stays unwatched. **Mark as watched** marks it watched too.

## Can new episodes download by themselves?

Yes, but it is off by default. See [Automatic downloads](/docs/guides/downloads#automatic-downloads).

## Do I get a notification?

Yes, a desktop notification groups new episodes ("5 new episodes from 3 anime"). Clicking it opens the Updates page.
