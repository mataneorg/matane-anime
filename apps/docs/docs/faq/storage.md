# Storage

## Where is my data?

In the app's user data folder (library, progress, settings, covers of your library) and your download folder. Uninstalling does not delete them. See [Install](/docs/guides/install#first-launch).

## How do I uninstall completely?

Uninstall the app, then delete the user data folder and your download folder. Use [backup and restore](/docs/guides/backups) first if you want to keep a copy.

## What takes up space?

Mostly downloaded episodes. **Settings, Data and storage** shows how much they use against your size limit, with **Open folder**. The cover cache holds covers from browsing and search; set its size limit or **Clear cache** there. Covers of your library are never removed.

## What is in a backup?

Library, categories, progress, history, watch sessions, settings, repositories, the list of installed extensions and library covers. Not in it: passwords, downloaded episodes, extension code, the image cache, anime you only browsed, and incognito state. See [Backups](/docs/guides/backups#what-is-in-a-backup).

## How do I move to another computer?

Make a backup on the old one, install the app on the new one, and **Restore**. Reinstall your extensions (they are marked **needs reinstall**) and copy your downloads by hand if you want them. See [Restore](/docs/guides/backups#restore).

## How do I clear the history without losing progress?

**Settings, Data and storage, Clear history**, or **Clear all history** on the History page. Progress and watch time stay. **Clear watch time** resets [statistics](/docs/guides/statistics#clearing) and keeps history and progress.

## Are my proxy password and credentials backed up?

No. A proxy password is stored in your system keyring when there is one (and is never shown again); passwords and tokens are not in a backup.
