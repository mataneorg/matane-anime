# Browse

## What is Browse?

The place to look through a source, search it, or search every source at once (**Global search**). See [Getting started](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-anime-to-your-library) and [Library](/docs/guides/library#adding-anime).

## Why is Browse empty?

The app ships with **no sources**. Add an extension first: [Adding sources](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-sources).

## Can I change how a source's list looks?

Yes: comfortable grid, compact grid, covers only or a list, with a cover size slider. The choice is remembered.

## Why does a search find nothing?

Try another spelling, a shorter title, or another source ([tips](/docs/guides/getting-started#trouble-finding-a-specific-anime)). Searching needs a connection.

## Why does a source ask for a "verification"?

The site shows an anti-bot check. Choose **Solve check**; see [Network](/docs/guides/network#cloudflare-challenges).

## I added an anime by mistake. How do I remove it?

Open it and use the library button, or right-click it in the library. Its progress and history stay if you add it again.
