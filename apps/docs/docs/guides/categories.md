# Categories

Categories group your library the way you like: by what you are in the mood for, by season, by who you watch with. They are optional. An anime can be in several categories at once, or in none.

The library shows an **All** tab with everything, a tab per category and, once you have at least one category, a **Default** tab with the anime that are in none.

## Making and managing categories

Open **Settings, Library, Categories** to:

- **Add** a category with a name. The **+** button beside the tabs in the library does the same.
- **Rename** it.
- **Move** it up or down to change the order of the tabs.
- **Delete** it. The anime in it stay in your library.

You can also right-click a category tab in the library to rename it, move it left or right, turn **Auto-download new episodes** on or off for it, or delete it.

## Putting anime in a category

- On an anime's page, or from the context menu in Browse and the library: **Move to category…** / **Add to category…**.
- In the library, **Select** several anime and choose **Move to category**.

## Using categories

<Shot name="library" alt="Categories appear as tabs above the library" />

- Each category is a **tab** in the library. The tab you last used is not remembered after you close the app.
- Library and category [new-episode checks](/docs/guides/updates) can run for one category only.
- [Auto-download](/docs/guides/downloads#automatic-downloads) can include or exclude categories, and **Settings, Downloads** can keep the downloads of some categories after you watch them.
- The command palette can jump to anime in any category.
- Categories are part of a [backup](/docs/guides/backups).
