# Library

The library is the anime you chose to keep, with your progress in each.

## Adding anime

Open **Browse**, pick a source and look through it, or search it. **Browse, Global search** searches every installed source at once and shows the results grouped by source; the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) can start the same search. See [Getting started](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-anime-to-your-library) for the steps.

On an anime's page, **Add to library** keeps it, and removing it works the same way. You can also right-click an anime in Browse and choose **Add to category…**. Covers of library anime are kept on disk, so the library looks the same offline, and they are refreshed when the metadata is refreshed.

## Organising

<Shot name="library" alt="The library with its category tabs, filters and display options" />

- **Categories** appear as tabs. An anime can be in several at once, or in none; see [Categories](/docs/guides/categories).
- **Display:** comfortable grid, compact grid, covers only or a list. The grid displays have a **Cover size** slider (100 to 280 px), and a badge on each cover shows how many episodes you have not watched. A source's list in Browse has the same choices. The display, size, sort and filters are remembered when you close the app; the category tab and the search text are not.
- **Filter** by unwatched, started, downloaded, status or source, and **sort** by title, last watched, latest episode, date added or number of unwatched episodes. **Reverse order** flips the direction.
- The search box filters the current view by title and alternative titles.
- **Select** several anime to move them to another category, mark them watched, download them or remove them.
- **Continue watching** on a card opens the right episode ([Watching and progress](/docs/guides/watching)).

## On an anime's page

<Shot name="anime" alt="An anime's page with its episode list" />

The page lists the episodes with their state: **Watched**, **Downloaded** and, when a source dropped an episode you had watched, started, downloaded or in history, **Removed by source** (the app keeps it).

- **Start watching** or **Continue** opens the episode to watch next.
- **Sort** the list newest or oldest first and filter it by unwatched or downloaded; the choice is remembered for that anime.
- Right-click an episode (or use its menu) to mark it watched or not watched, **mark all previous as watched**, **reset progress**, download it, or delete its download.
- Select several episodes to download, mark or delete them together.
- **Refresh** asks the source again. If the source cannot be reached, the app shows what it saved earlier.
- **Open in browser** opens the anime's page on the source's website.

## Progress and history

Matane Anime remembers where you stopped in every episode. **History** lists what you watched recently ([Watching and progress](/docs/guides/watching)). To watch without leaving a trace, use [Incognito](/docs/guides/incognito). For numbers over time, see [Statistics](/docs/guides/statistics).
